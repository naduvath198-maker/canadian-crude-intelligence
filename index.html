Evidence & reproducibility / appendix

Data Sources & Methodology

A transparent data trail matters as much as the charts. The demo observations are not actual market prices and must be replaced before using the analysis in an application.

Replace demonstration values with sourced monthly data

Prepare a CSV with headers Month,WTI_USD_bbl,WCS_USD_bbl,USD_CAD and one row per complete month, such as 2026-09,72.00,55.00,1.3900 (example only). Ensure dates, units, averaging methods and price definitions align across sources.

Browser imports work locally and do not change the public website. To update the public dashboard, upload a verified CSV named market_data.csv to the repository root; no data is uploaded to a server. Imported values are not automatically verified.

Methodology & limitations

01 / MEASURENormalize market data

Align matching months, ensure USD/bbl units for both crude benchmarks and CAD per USD for FX.

02 / ANALYZECalculate differential

WCS discount = WTI − WCS. Percentage discount = price differential divided by WTI.

03 / SIMULATETranslate to a client case

Apply hypothetical production, time period, FX and benchmark prices to indicative gross revenue.
